Ingredients
  • 4 cod fillets, about 6 ounces each
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • To taste Salt and Pepper
  • 1 sliced lemon
  • A few Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
Procedure
  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Place the cod fillets in a baking dish.
  3. In a small bowl, mix together the olive oil, thyme, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Drizzle the mixture over the cod fillets, making sure they are evenly coated.
  5. Place a few slices of lemon on top of each fillet.
  6. Bake in the oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the cod is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
  7. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
