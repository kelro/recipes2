Ground Turkey and Vegetable Skillet

One-pan dinner with Turkey, Vegetables, and Italian Seasoning

Ingredients
  • 1 lb Ground Turkey (93% recommended)
  • 1 medium (about 7 oz) Zucchini, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
  • 1 Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 Green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small Yellow Onion, diced
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) Diced Tomatoes, with juices
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 1/4 cup Water or low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 1/2 tsp Italian Seasoning
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1/2 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 1/2 tsp Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp Crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1/4 cup Freshly grated Paramesan cheeshe
  • 2 tbsp Chopped fresh parsley or basil
Procedure
  1. Dice the onion and bell peppers into roughly ½-inch pieces. Cut zucchini into ½-inch half-moons or cubes. Mince the garlic. Keep the zucchini separate because it cooks faster than the peppers.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and break it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook until lightly browned, about 6–8 minutes. Transfer to a plate. It will finish cooking later.
  3. Add the remaining tablespoon of olive oil to the skillet. Add diced onion and both bell peppers. Sauté over medium heat for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened.
  4. Add the zucchini and cook for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add the minced garlic and cook another 30 seconds until fragrant. The zucchini should begin to soften but still hold its shape.
  5. Return the ground turkey and any accumulated juices to the skillet. Stir in tomato paste, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, pepper, and optional red pepper flakes. Add diced tomatoes with their juices and ¼ cup chicken broth. Mix thoroughly.
  6. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally. Check that the ground turkey reaches an internal temperature of 165°F. Taste and adjust salt or pepper. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley or basil before serving.
Notes
Refrigerate leftovers within two hours in an airtight container. They will keep for 3–4 days. Reheat to 165°F, adding a tablespoon or two of water if the mixture seems dryMy favorite flavor upgrade
For a slightly richer, Italian-style skillet, add 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce and ¼ cup shredded mozzarella during the final two minutes of cooking. Cover briefly to melt the cheese.

This is especially good served over rice, pasta, or mashed potatoes.
