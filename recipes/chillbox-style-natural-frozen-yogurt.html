Ingredients
  • 900 g (about 3¾ cups) low-fat Greek yogurt
  • 110 g (about ½ cup) fructose
  • 125 mL (½ cup) 2% milk
Procedure
  1. Whisk everything together until completely smooth.
  2. Refrigerate the mixture for at least 1 hour and up to 4 hours.
  3. Put it into an ice-cream maker and churn for approximately 10–12 minutes, stopping when it reaches that smooth, creamy soft-serve consistency you remember from Chillbox.
Notes
Don't substitute ordinary granulated sugar initially. The Chillbox-attributed recipe specifically calls for fructose.
